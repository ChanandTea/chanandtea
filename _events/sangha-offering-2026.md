body_en: |-
  <p>On 22 August 2026, lay devotees offered a meal to the Sangha at Ling Yen Mountain Temple in Canada, and everything was perfect.</p>
  <p>Perfection carries a deep meaning in Buddhist terms. It means that all the causes and conditions for the offering came together without deficiency: the sincerity of those who gave, the gift that was offered, and the Sangha who received it were all present and pure in intent. When giver, gift, and receiver come together in this way, the merit is perfected, and it is dedicated to the benefit of all beings. This perfection rests on the emptiness of the three wheels: the giver, the gift, and the receiver. None of the three has a fixed, independent self to cling to. The giver does not hold on to the thought 'I am giving,' the gift is not grasped as 'mine,' and the receiver is not singled out as 'other.' Giving is done without attachment to any of the three, without seeking reward, praise, or return, and without discriminating between high and low. Because nothing is clung to, the offering is pure, and its merit is immeasurable.</p>
  <h2>Seated together</h2>
  <p>Under the lanterns, monastics sat together at long tables while lay friends served the meal with care and reverence. The hall was full, and the atmosphere was calm and joyful. For the Sangha, to receive such an offering is to receive the devotion of the community itself.</p>
  <h2>An offering in the Ullambana season</h2>
  <p>The assembly was held in the Ullambana season, when gratitude to parents and ancestors is remembered. To offer food to the Sangha at such a time is an act of deep merit, and the lay devotees offered with whole hearts. The meal was a fine and generous one, prepared and served with great attention.</p>
  <h2>Two associations, one Sangha</h2>
  <p>Venerable Daojing attended representing two organisations, Chan and Tea Culture International and the International Chan Cultural Research Association, as Vice-President. Venerable Daojing was accompanied by volunteers William Su, Eva Huang, and Michelle Yang. Eva drove the group to Ling Yen Mountain Temple in Canada. A donation of $2,000 was received, and Venerable Daojing is grateful for it.</p>
  <h2>Thanks to everybody</h2>
  <p>Thank you to everybody — to all the volunteers, to the donors, and to everyone who helped in any way to make the day what it was. Every part of the gathering came together in harmony, and the joy was shared by monastics and lay friends alike.</p>
  <p>May the merit of this offering benefit all beings. Chan and Tea Culture International and the International Chan Cultural Research Association will continue to support the study, practice, and sharing of Chan culture, and gatherings where monastics and lay friends meet in gratitude and harmony.</p>

  <p>2026年8月22日，在俗信眾於加拿大靈巖山寺向僧團供齋，一切都圓滿。</p>
  <p>圓滿在佛教中有著深刻的含義。它意味著供養的種種因緣毫無欠缺地匯聚在一起：布施者的誠心、所施之物，以及接受供養的僧團，都具足並且發心清淨。當施者、施物與受者如此匯聚時，功德得以圓滿，並迴向利益一切眾生。這種圓滿建立在三輪體空之上：施者、施物與受者這三輪。沒有一輪具有固定、獨立的自我可以執著。施者不執著「我在布施」的念頭，施物不被執為「我的」，受者也不被分別為「他者」。布施時不執著於三輪中的任何一輪，不求回報、讚譽或回饋，也不分別高下。因為無所執著，供養清淨，其功德無可衡量。</p>
  <h2>同坐一堂</h2>
  <p>燈籠之下，僧眾在長桌旁同坐，在家信眾以細心與恭敬侍奉齋飯。會場滿座，氣氛寧靜祥和。對僧團而言，接受這樣的供養，就是接受信眾團體的虔誠。</p>
  <h2>盂蘭盆節的供養</h2>
  <p>此次法會於盂蘭盆節期間舉行，正值憶念父母與祖先恩德之時。在此時節向僧團供食，是深具功德之舉，在家信眾以全心全意供養。齋飯豐盛可口，以極大的細心預備與奉上。</p>
  <h2>兩個機構，一個僧團</h2>
  <p>道憬法師以副會長身分，代表國際禪茶文化聯合會與國際禪文化研究會這兩個機構出席此次法會。陪同道憬法師前來的義工有 William Su、Eva Huang 和 Michelle Yang。Eva 駕車載一行人前往加拿大靈巖山寺。此次法會收到捐款$2,000，道憬法師對此深表感謝。</p>
  <h2>感謝大家</h2>
  <p>感謝大家——感謝所有義工、捐助者，以及以任何方式協助成就這一天的每一個人。聚會的每一個環節都和諧匯聚，僧俗朋友共同分享了這份喜悅。</p>
  <p>願此次供養的功德利益一切眾生。國際禪茶文化聯合會與國際禪文化研究會將繼續支持禪文化的學習、修習與分享，以及僧俗在感恩與和諧中相聚的聚會。</p>
