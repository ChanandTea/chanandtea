  <h2>Before the Gathering: Planning Meetings</h2>
  <p>Before the gathering on Parliament Hill, planning meetings were held on 20 April and 15 May 2026.</p>
  <p>Before the gathering, various venerables and esteemed masters met to talk over the event, including Venerable Dayi, Abbot of Cham Shan Temple and President of the Buddhist Association of Canada, and Tsengdok Rinpoche; both masters and their temples were among the six main organizers of the event.</p>
  <p>Venerable Dayi spoke on the importance of being respectful to parents and having benevolence towards sentient beings, and Tsengdok Rinpoche spoke on the importance of Buddhist Heritage Month and on the three traditions — Theravada, Mahayana and Tibetan — working together in Canada.</p>
  <p>On 25 May 2026, more than 1,000 participants and 150 monastics from across North America gathered on Parliament Hill in Ottawa for a historic assembly advocating for the official federal designation of May as Canadian Buddhist Heritage Month.</p>
  <h2>A broad coalition</h2>
  <p>The landmark gathering was organized by a broad coalition of Theravada, Mahayana, and Tibetan traditions, and marked the most significant public assembly to date supporting the movement. The initiative seeks formal cultural recognition from the federal government to honour the historic contributions of over 500,000 Buddhist Canadians. Organizers emphasized that the movement is rooted in a desire for cultural visibility and inclusion, rather than religious privilege.</p>
  <h2>The day’s events</h2>
  <p>The day's events began with monastics attending proceedings in Canada's House of Commons, followed by a joint press conference at West Block. Public activities in front of the Centennial Flame included the traditional Buddha bathing ceremony and a peace walk. There was also the first display of the Buddha's relics in front of Parliament.</p>
  <h2>Chan and Tea’s part</h2>
  <p>As an active participating NGO, Chan and Tea Culture International stood proudly alongside community leaders and practitioners to amplify this collective call for recognition. The gathering highlighted how deeply the core Buddhist values of mindfulness, compassion, and peace are woven into the fabric of Canadian civic culture. Nine members from Chan and Tea Culture International took part in the event as volunteers, led by Venerable Daojing, Vice-President of the association. Venerable Daojing attended representing both organisations — Chan and Tea Culture International and the International Chan Cultural Research Association — as Vice-President.</p>
  <h2>A growing movement</h2>
  <p>The movement is also finding resonance across the country, echoing grassroots efforts in six provinces, and is currently under legislative consideration. The association will continue to give its full support to this national movement, and extends its deepest gratitude to all the participants and volunteers who travelled to Ottawa to speak out for the community's shared vision.</p>
  <p>Chan and Tea Culture International is dedicated to promoting Chan — the Chan school of Chinese Buddhism — and Chinese tea culture. Through a cup of tea, the association hopes to bring stillness, compassion and harmony to all: a bowl of tea, quietly shared, can help the mind settle, open the heart, and bring people together in peace.</p>

  <h2>集結之前：籌備會議</h2>
  <p>在國會山莊集結之前，籌備會議於2026年4月20日與5月15日舉行。</p>
  <p>事前，多位法師及大德會面，商討此次活動。其中包括達義法師——湛山精舍住持、加拿大佛教會會長——與參智仁波切，兩位法師及其道場均為此次活動的六個主要主辦單位之一。</p>
  <p>達義法師開示了孝敬父母與慈悲眾生的重要性；參智仁波切則談及佛教文化遺產月的重要性，以及南傳、漢傳與藏傳三大傳統在加拿大攜手合作。</p>
  <p>2026年5月25日，來自北美各地的一千多名參加者與一百五十位法師，齊聚渥太華國會山莊，參加一場具有歷史意義的集會，呼籲聯邦政府正式將五月定為加拿大佛教文化遺產月。</p>
  <h2>廣泛的聯盟</h2>
  <p>這場具有里程碑意義的集會，由南傳、漢傳與藏傳佛教的廣泛聯盟共同主辦，是迄今為止支持這項運動的最具規模的公開集會。這項倡議旨在爭取聯邦政府的正式文化認可，以表彰五十多萬加拿大佛教徒的歷史貢獻。主辦方強調，這項運動植根於對文化能見度與包容，而非宗教特權。</p>
  <h2>當天的活動</h2>
  <p>當天的活動由法師們出席加拿大國會下議院的議程揭開序幕，隨後在西座（West Block）舉行聯合記者會。在百年聖火前的公開活動，包括傳統浴佛儀式及和平步行。另外也有首次佛陀舍利子在國會前的展示。</p>
  <h2>禪茶會的參與</h2>
  <p>作為積極參與的非政府組織，國際禪茶文化聯合會與社區領袖及修行者並肩站在一起，壯大這項集體認可的呼聲。這次集會彰顯了正念、慈悲與和平等佛教核心價值，如何深深融入加拿大公民文化的肌理。國際禪茶文化聯合會共有九位成員以義工身分參與此次活動，由該會副會長道憬法師率領。 道憬法師以副會長身份，同時代表國際禪茶文化聯合會與國際禪文化研究會兩個組織出席。</p>
  <h2>不斷壯大的運動</h2>
  <p>這項運動也在各地獲得迴響，與六個省份的基層努力相互呼應，目前仍在立法審議中。本會將繼續全力支持這項全國運動，並向所有前往渥太華、為社區共同願景發聲的參加者與義工，致以最深切的感謝。</p>
