body_en: |-
  <p>Members of Chan and Tea Culture International learned to decorate a temple in Vancouver, BC, Canada to mark its twentieth anniversary, in a gathering shaped by flower arranging, shared work and listening to the Dharma.</p>
  <p>The gathering had a simple aim: to grow in mutual understanding and coordination. The group assisted the temple's volunteers, arranged flowers, set up the venue, and listened to a Dharma talk.</p>
  <p>Everyone was filled with the joy of the Dharma.</p>
  <p>Chan and Tea Culture International promotes Chan — the Chinese Buddhist Chan school — and Chinese tea culture. Through tea, the association offers a way into stillness, compassion and harmony: a quiet bowl of tea, shared with others, can help the mind settle, open the heart, and bring people together in peace.</p>
body_zh: |-
  <p>國際禪茶文化聯合會的成員在溫哥華當地一寺院協助佈置，慶祝寺院二十周年。聚會以插花、共同工作和聽聞法師開示為主。</p>
  <p>當天有一個簡單的目標：培養默契。大家協助寺院的義工菩薩，插花，佈置場地，並聽法師開示。</p>
  <p>大家法喜充滿。</p>
  <p>國際禪茶文化聯合會致力於弘揚禪——中國佛教禪宗——與中國茶文化。透過一杯茶，協會希望為大眾帶來寧靜、慈悲與和諧：一碗靜靜分享的茶，可以幫助心安定下來，打開心胸，讓人們在和平中共聚。</p>
