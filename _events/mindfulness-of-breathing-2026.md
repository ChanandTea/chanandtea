body_en: |-
  <p>On 30 May 2026, students shared a verse of gratitude for the teaching of Venerable Kaiyin (開印法師), at a discussion with Venerable Kaiyin and Lin Songyuan (林松源).</p>
  <h2>A student's verse</h2>
  <p>The verse, shared by students in thanks for Venerable Kaiyin's teaching, reads in translation:</p>
  <p>“In Vancouver the Dharma is supreme; at Wuxianju the sound of the Dharma is played. In the Yogācāra grounds, mindfulness of breathing; one-pointedness of mind, the nine abidings. Seeing one's nature, realising the sacred truths; grateful to our teachers and guides. Rejoicing and praising, all benefit together; blessings and wisdom grow day by day; life after life, may we give rise to the bodhi mind! We kneel and ask our teachers to come again.”</p>
  <p>The verse speaks of mindfulness of breathing, of a mind gathered to one point, and of seeing one's nature and realising the sacred truths — and it closes by asking, with gratitude, for the teachers to come again.</p>
  <h2>A poem quoted by the students</h2>
  <p>Alongside their verse, the students quoted Su Shi's (蘇軾) “Poem on the Lute” (《琴詩》):</p>
  <p>“If you say the sound is on the lute, why does it not sound when it lies in its case? If you say the sound is in the fingers, why do you not hear it on your fingers?”</p>

body_zh: |-
  <p>2026年5月30日，學員們分享了一首感恩詩，感謝開印法師的教導。當天是開印法師與林松源的座談。</p>
  <h2>學員分享</h2>
  <p>學員們感謝開印阿闍梨教導的分享如下：</p>
  <p>「溫哥華中法殊勝，無弦居中彈法音，瑜伽師地安般念，心一境性九心住，明心見性悟聖諦，感恩師父老師導，隨喜讚嘆同受益，一切福慧皆增長，世世永發菩提心！跪求師輩再次來。」</p>
  <p>這首詩談及安般念、心一境性，以及明心見性、悟入聖諦，並以感恩之心，懇請師輩再次前來。</p>
  <h2>學員引用的詩</h2>
  <p>學員們同時引用了蘇軾的《琴詩》：</p>
  <p>「若言琴上有琴聲，放在匣中何不鳴？若言聲在指頭上，何不於君指上聽？」</p>
