body_en: |-
  <p>From 18 to 21 March 2026, members of Chan and Tea Culture International (加拿大國際禪茶文化聯合會) gathered at Qualicum Beach, on Vancouver Island, for a wellness retreat of mindful climbing and practice, led by Peter to his future meditation centre.</p>
  <h2>The programme</h2>
  <p>Mornings began indoors with yoga and standing-post practice. At midday the group climbed outdoors to the hilltop, practising awareness through the eyes and experiencing the present moment, and by the sea practised deep, attentive listening, in the spirit of ear-root awakening (耳根圓通).</p>
  <p>Afternoons were given to discussing the Dharma and drinking tea, and to Regina and Cindy sharing their life stories, drawing out lessons and reflecting together on the best way to practise. On the third day the group sat in meditation for twelve hours.</p>
  <p>Participants carried the spirit of the retreat in their hearts, looking forward to meeting again.</p>
  <h2>Place and participants</h2>
  <p>The retreat was held at 2300 Fowler Road, Qualicum Beach, Vancouver Island, British Columbia. Those named as taking part were Peter, Cindy, Regina, Angie and Jocelyn.</p>
  <p>Chan and Tea Culture International promotes Chan — the Chinese Buddhist Chan school — and Chinese tea culture. Through tea, the association offers a way into stillness, compassion and harmony: a quiet bowl of tea, shared with others, can help the mind settle, open the heart, and bring people together in peace.</p>
body_zh: |-
  <p>2026年3月18日至21日，加拿大國際禪茶文化聯合會的成員齊聚溫哥華島Qualicum Beach，參加練心爬山養生活動，由Peter帶領大家前往他未來的禪修中心。</p>
  <h2>活動流程</h2>
  <p>早上在室內做瑜伽和站樁。中午到戶外登山頂，練習眼根覺受，體驗當下；在海邊練習耳根圓通，專注聆聽。</p>
  <p>下午討論佛法、喝茶，Regina和Cindy分享人生歷程，總結經驗，共同參悟最佳修行方法。第三天打坐十二小時。</p>
  <p>大家帶上心中的精神，期待下次相見。</p>
  <h2>地點與參加人員</h2>
  <p>活動地點：加拿大卑詩省溫哥華島Qualicum Beach, 2300 Fowler Road。參加人員：Peter、Cindy、Regina、Angie、Jocelyn。</p>
  <p>國際禪茶文化聯合會致力於弘揚禪——中國佛教禪宗——與中國茶文化。透過一杯茶，協會希望為大眾帶來寧靜、慈悲與和諧：一碗靜靜分享的茶，可以幫助心安定下來，打開心胸，讓人們在和平中共聚。</p>
