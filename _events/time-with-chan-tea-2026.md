body_en: |-
  <p>“Everyday is a good time for tea!” With those words, Venerable Dao Jing, Vice-President of Chan and Tea Culture International, shared some wise words on Chan and tea in connection with the association's gathering on 13 June 2026.</p>
  <h2>The art of presence</h2>
  <p>In a fast-paced world, the simple act of sharing a cup of tea can be a profound anchor. The association often speaks of tradition, but the true heart of its mission is found in the stillness that ripples outward from a single session of Chan and tea.</p>
  <p>“Everybody at peace and harmony” is something we have to realise and find within ourselves — our mind.</p>
  <p>The reflection sets out a vision and a goal: a world in which the quiet warmth of a single bowl of tea brings us to be mindful of the present moment, where there is only compassion and wisdom. “May sentient beings live in harmony and be at peace, and ultimate realisation of Buddhahood is not far!”</p>
  <p>Chan and Tea Culture International promotes Chan — the Chinese Buddhist Chan school — and Chinese tea culture. Through tea, the association offers a way into stillness, compassion and harmony: a quiet bowl of tea, shared with others, can help the mind settle, open the heart, and bring people together in peace.</p>
body_zh: |-
  <p>「每天都是喝茶的好時機！」國際禪茶文化聯合會副會長道憬法師以這句話開頭，分享了禪茶的智慧在2026年6月13日的聚會中。</p>
  <h2>活在當下</h2>
  <p>在節奏急速的世界裡，與人分享一杯茶這個簡單的舉動，可以成為深刻的依靠。協會常常談及傳統，但使命的真正核心，在於從一席禪茶中向外漣漪般擴散的寧靜。</p>
  <p>「人人和平和諧」，是我們必須在自己內心——我們的心——中體悟和尋得的。</p>
  <p>道憬法師希望一碗茶的靜靜暖意，能讓我們安住於當下，只有慈悲與智慧。「願眾生和諧共處、平安吉祥，究竟證悟佛果並不遙遠！」</p>
  <p>國際禪茶文化聯合會致力於弘揚禪——中國佛教禪宗——與中國茶文化。透過一杯茶，協會希望為大眾帶來寧靜、慈悲與和諧：一碗靜靜分享的茶，可以幫助心安定下來，打開心胸，讓人們在和平中共聚。</p>
