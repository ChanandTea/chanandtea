Purposes宗旨

As set out in the filed Articles of Incorporation.

  1. 01Purpose: To share and to promote Chan Tea culture.宗旨：分享与弘扬禅茶文化。
  2. 02Restrictions on activities: None.活动限制：无。
  3. 03Classes of members: One class; each member is entitled to receive notice of, attend and vote at all meetings of the members.会员：会员制度将于下届年度会员大会定稿，设两类会员。
  4. 04Distribution of property on liquidation: Any property remaining after discharge of liabilities shall be distributed to one or more qualified donees within the meaning of subsection 248(1) of the Income Tax Act.清算：在清偿债务后剩余的任何财产，应分配给《所得税法》第248(1)款所指的一家或多家合资格受赠机构（qualified donees）。

Activity Guidelines活动限制

This is the association’s own guideline for its activities. It is not a filed legal restriction — in the filed Articles, Restrictions on activities: None.

本节为本会的活动指引，并非已备案的法律限制。

When Venerables are present有法师在场时

If Venerables and Buddhist masters are present, they lead Chan meditation methods (Tiantai, Chan School, etc.) and give Buddhadharma talks.

如有法师与佛教大师在场，请由他们带领禅修方法（天台、禅宗等），并作佛法开示。

Laypersons-only events仅限居士参加的活动

For laypersons-only events, please do tea or culture-related sessions. Dharma talks, and leading Chan meditation should only be done by Venerables.

若为仅限居士参加的活动，请进行与茶或文化相关的活动。佛法开示和带领禅修应仅由法师进行。
