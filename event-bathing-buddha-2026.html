Chan Tea at the Buddha’s Birthday: Bathing Buddha at Tung Lin Kok Yuen, 23–24 May 2026佛誕禪茶——東蓮覺苑浴佛節，2026年5月23–24日

During the Buddha’s Birthday celebrations at Tung Lin Kok Yuen (東蓮覺苑), Chan and Tea volunteers joined the temple’s volunteers to offer Chan Tea to visitors who had come to celebrate the Buddha’s birthday.

Preparing for Chan Tea

At the venue, the Chan and Tea team helped to arrange the tables to get ready for Chan Tea. They worked alongside Tung Lin Kok Yuen’s volunteers and showed them how to run a Chan Tea meditation session.

The meditation session

The session was led by Venerable Huiliang. While it was happening, volunteers made tea in the traditional Chan monastery way, carried over from China, and gave tea to all the visitors who had come to celebrate the Buddha’s birthday.

Merit and connection

Everybody made merit and was able to connect with sentient beings and fulfil their bodhisattva vows.

More photographs

Chan and Tea Culture International promotes Chan — the Chinese Buddhist Chan school — and Chinese tea culture. Through tea, the association offers a way into stillness, compassion and harmony: a quiet bowl of tea, shared with others, can help the mind settle, open the heart, and bring people together in peace.

佛誕期間，禪茶義工與東蓮覺苑的義工一起，在東蓮覺苑為前來慶祝佛誕的訪客奉上禪茶。

準備禪茶

在會場，禪茶團隊協助擺設桌椅，為禪茶作準備。他們與東蓮覺苑的義工一起工作，並教導義工如何主持禪茶禪修。

禪修環節

禪修由慧量法師帶領。禪修進行期間，義工以源自中國的傳統禪宗寺院方式泡茶，並將茶奉給所有前來慶祝佛誕的訪客。

功德與結緣

人人皆種功德，得以與眾生結緣，成就菩薩願行。

國際禪茶文化聯合會致力於弘揚禪——中國佛教禪宗——與中國茶文化。透過一杯茶，協會希望為大眾帶來寧靜、慈悲與和諧：一碗靜靜分享的茶，可以幫助心安定下來，打開心胸，讓人們在和平中共聚。
