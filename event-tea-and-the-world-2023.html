WUYISHAN, FUJIAN — 16–18 November 2023福建武夷山——2023年11月16–18日

Tea and the World: The 2023 Wuyishan Tea and the World Forum, 16–18 November 2023茶和天下：2023武夷山茶和天下论坛，2023年11月16–18日

Under the theme “One mountain, one water, one leaf; one Belt and Road, one pot of tea”, the 2023 Wuyishan “Tea and the World” Forum was held in Wuyishan from 16 to 18 November 2023, bringing together more than four hundred guests from cultural, tea and business circles, from both sides of the Taiwan Strait and from France, the United States, Canada and Australia.

Wuyishan, a UNESCO World Cultural and Natural Heritage site and a beautiful national park, hosted the gathering. The forum was organised by the Fujian Chan Tea Culture Exchange Association and the Wuyishan Tianxin Zhizi Culture Exchange Centre, with tea as the medium and harmony as the guiding value, and with the spirit of “uprightness, clarity, harmony and elegance” (正清和雅) at its heart.

A luminous pearl, and a new centre

On the morning of 16 November, a ceremony at the Dahongpao ancestral temple, Tianxin Yongle Chan Temple (天心永樂禪寺), marked the donation and installation of a luminous pearl by the collector Zhuang Zhiming (莊志銘) to the temple, in the presence of Abbot Zedao (澤道大和尚) and more than a hundred guests. At ten o'clock the same morning, the Wuyishan Zhizi Culture Exchange Centre was unveiled at the temple, to provide a shared platform for Confucian, Buddhist, Daoist, Zhu Xi and tea cultures in a mountain where the three teachings have long met.

The opening ceremony

The forum's opening ceremony was held on the morning of 17 November, with provincial and municipal leaders in attendance, including Liu Xianxiang (劉獻祥), Vice-Chair of the Fujian Provincial Political Consultative Conference, who declared the forum open, and Lin Zhongle (林鍾樂) and Zhang Zhongfa (張忠發), who spoke in congratulation. Scholars and cultural figures from Peking University, Renmin University, Nanjing University, National Taiwan University and Fu Jen Catholic University were among those present, together with artists, tea masters and guests from overseas Chan tea associations.

Greetings were offered by guests from France, Canada, Australia and the United States, including Janusz Kuras (賈努斯·庫拉斯), Vice-President and Secretary-General of the Canadian International Chan Tea Federation, carrying a message of friendship and of tea as a bond between peoples. Outside, thirty-two local tea companies set out open-air tea seats at the Wuyi Tea Expo Park, so that guests could taste tea at close hand and experience the character of Wuyi tea — described in the report as “rock bone and floral fragrance, formed by nature”.

The forum

That afternoon, the forum itself met at the Taiwei Golf Hotel in Wuyishan, on the theme of a single leaf and a single pot of tea bringing harmony of heart, harmony of family, harmony of society, shared roots across the Strait, and peace in the world. Keynote addresses were given by Professor Wang Shouchang (王守常) of Peking University, who spoke of tradition as alive in the present; Academician Xiong Chengyu (熊澄宇); Professor Li Zhonghua (李中華); Professor Liu Zongmin (劉宗民) of Fu Jen Catholic University, who set out the shared history of Taiwan and Fujian tea; and Vice-Dean Zheng Shi (鄭適) of Renmin University, who spoke of the forum's theme from the perspectives of economics and culture.

Two panel discussions followed. The first, chaired by Professor Wang Shouchang, took “harmony of heart, harmony of family, harmony of society” as its theme; the second, chaired by Professor Yang Jun (楊駿) of Nanjing University and including Janusz Kuras, took “shared roots across the Strait, world peace, and tea and the world” as its theme. The forum ended in a warm and friendly atmosphere, with the wish that tea — “a small boat” carrying hopes of peace — should carry the blessing of “tea and the world” far beyond the green waters and red mountains of Wuyishan.

Chan and Tea Culture International promotes Chan — the Chinese Buddhist Chan school — and Chinese tea culture. Through tea, the association offers a way into stillness, compassion and harmony: a quiet bowl of tea, shared with others, can help the mind settle, open the heart, and bring people together in peace.

2023年11月16日至18日，以“一山一水一片叶，一带一路一壶茶”为主题的“2023武夷山茶和天下论坛”在武夷山隆重举行，来自文化界、茶学界、企业界，海峡两岸及法国、美国、加拿大、澳大利亚等四百多海内外友人出席盛会。

武夷山是世界文化与自然双遗产地和美丽的国家公园。本届论坛由福建省禅茶文化交流协会、武夷山市天心诸子文化交流中心主办，以茶为媒，以和为尚，共同感受“正清和雅”的茶文化，发扬武夷山“感恩、包容、分享”的茶理念，表达“茶和天下”的美好祝愿。

夜明珠捐赠与诸子中心揭幕

11月16日上午9时，捐赠夜明珠安放仪式在大红袍祖庭天心永乐禅寺隆重举行，全球著名夜明珠收藏家、上海梦想成真公益基金会夜明珠文化专项基金主任庄志铭先生向天心永乐禅寺捐赠夜明珠，泽道大和尚及百余名嘉宾参加仪式。上午10时，武夷山市诸子文化交流中心在天心永乐禅寺揭幕，旨在为儒、释、道及朱子文化、茶文化等各家文化提供一个共同交流的平台。

开幕式

11月17日上午，2023武夷山茶和天下论坛开幕式隆重举行。福建省政协副主席刘献祥宣布论坛正式开幕，海峡两岸茶叶交流协会会长林钟乐、福建省民族与宗教事务厅副厅长张忠发先后发言致贺。文化界、茶界和艺术界人士出席，包括北京大学、南京大学、台湾大学、辅仁大学的学者，以及法国、加拿大、澳大利亚、美国的嘉宾。

开幕式上，来自法国、加拿大、澳大利亚、美国的嘉宾表达热烈祝贺，其中包括加拿大国际禅茶文化联合会副会长兼秘书长贾努斯·库拉斯致词。中华武夷茶博园开展“茶和天下”主题户外茶席，由天心禅茶等32家本地企业摆设茶席，零距离感受茶文化的魅力，展现武夷茶“岩骨花香，自然天成”的神韵。

论坛

17日下午，2023武夷山茶和天下论坛在武夷山太伟高尔夫酒店正式举办，主题是“一片叶，一壶茶，人心和悦、家庭和睦、社会和谐、两岸合源、世界和平”。北京大学教授王守常、熊澄宇院士、李中华教授、辅仁大学教授刘宗民、中国人民大学副院长郑适分别作主旨发言。其中王守常教授提出传统就在当下的理念，刘宗民教授系统介绍了台湾茶与福建茶合源的历史，郑适院长从经济与文化的角度诠释茶和天下的现实意义。

论坛分为两组座谈：第一组由王守常教授主持，以“人心和悦、家庭和睦、社会和谐”为主题；第二组由南京大学杨骏教授主持，加拿大国际禅茶文化联合会副会长兼秘书长贾努斯·库拉斯等参加，以“两岸合源、世界和平、茶和天下”为主题。本届论坛在融洽友好的氛围下圆满结束，茶这片小小的舟楫载着和平的希望，飘向更辽阔的远方。

国际禅茶文化联合会致力于弘扬禅——中国佛教禅宗——与中国茶文化。通过一杯茶，协会希望为大众带来宁静、慈悲与和谐：一碗静静分享的茶，可以帮助心安定下来，打开心胸，让人们在和平中共聚。
