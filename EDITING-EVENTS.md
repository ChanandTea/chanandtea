# How to add or edit an event

You can add, change or remove events on the Chan and Tea Culture International website
through a simple online form. No code is needed. Changes appear on the website about one to two minutes after you save.

**Website:** https://chanandtea.github.io/chanandtea/
**Editor:** https://app.pagescms.org

---

## First time only: sign in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and sign in with the Chan and Tea GitHub account.
3. If asked to **install the GitHub App**, choose the **ChanandTea** organisation, then choose
   **Only select repositories → chanandtea**, and click **Install**.
4. Open the **chanandtea** repository. You will see **Events 活动** in the menu on the left.

After this, you only need to go to app.pagescms.org and you will already be signed in.

---

## Add a new event

1. Click **Events 活动** on the left, then **Add an entry** (or the **+** button).
2. Fill in the form. Fields marked with * are required:

   | Field | What to put |
   |---|---|
   | **Title (English)** * / **标题（中文）** * | Short name, e.g. *Winter Tea Gathering* / *冬季茶会* |
   | **Date (first day)** * | The day of the event. |
   | **End date** | Only for events lasting more than one day. Otherwise leave empty. |
   | **Time** (English / 中文) | Optional, e.g. *3:00–4:30 PM* / *下午3:00–4:30* |
   | **Place** (English / 中文) | e.g. *Tung Lin Kok Yuen, Vancouver* / *东莲觉苑* |
   | **Short summary** (English / 中文) | One or two sentences. Shown in the events list and on the home page. |
   | **Main photo** | Upload one photo. It appears at the top of the article and in the events list. |
   | **Article** (English / 中文) | The full write-up. Use the toolbar for headings, bold and links. |
   | **Chinese article is in Traditional characters** | Tick only if the Chinese article uses 繁體字. |
   | **More photos** | Click **Add** for each photo and give it a caption in both languages. |

3. Click **Save**. The event appears on the website in about one to two minutes.

**Upcoming events** are shown at the top of the Events page under *Coming up*.
After the event date passes, they move to *Past events* automatically, and the three most recent past events appear on the home page.

---

## Edit or remove an event

- **Edit:** click **Events 活动**, choose the event, change what you need, and click **Save**.
- **Remove:** open the event, click the **⋯** menu, and choose **Delete**.

## Tips

- If you leave the Chinese fields empty, the English text is shown in the Chinese version until you add it.
- Photos: JPG or PNG. Photos straight from a phone are fine, but smaller files (under about 1 MB) make the website faster.
- Mistakes can be undone: every change is saved in the website's history. Contact your web person if you need something restored.
- To check your change, open the website and refresh the page. If it hasn't appeared yet, wait a minute and refresh again.
