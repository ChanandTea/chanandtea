WUYISHAN, FUJIAN — 17 November 2024福建武夷山——2024年11月17日

Let the World See China's Dahongpao: The 12th Dahongpao Chan Tea Exchange, Wuyishan, 17 November 2024让世界看见中国大红袍：第十二届大红袍禅茶文化交流活动，2024年11月17日

On 17 November 2024, the twelfth Dahongpao Chan Tea Culture Exchange was held in Wuyishan under the theme “One mountain, one water, one leaf; one Belt and Road, one pot of tea”, under the title “Let the World See China's Dahongpao”. Guests from the United States, Canada, France, Japan, Germany, Australia and India joined compatriots from Taiwan and Macao, gathered in Wuyi with tea as the medium and harmony as the guiding value.

The event was hosted by the organising committee of the sixteenth Cross-Strait Tea Expo, guided by the Fujian, Nanping and Wuyishan ethnic and religious affairs departments, and organised by the Fujian Chan Tea Culture Exchange Association, the Wuyishan Tianxin Zhizi Culture Exchange Centre and the Wuyishan Chan Tea Culture Research Association. Wuyishan is the birthplace of oolong and black tea, the starting point of the Wanli Tea Road, and home of the Wuyi rock tea (Dahongpao) making skills, among the first Chinese tea-culture arts townships and a national intangible heritage.

Opening speeches

In his welcome address, Lin Zhongle (林鍾樂), former Vice-Chair of the Fujian Provincial Political Consultative Conference and President of the Cross-Strait Tea Exchange Association, said that Wuyi tea is no longer only a drink, but a symbol of Chinese culture and a “green business card” of the Belt and Road initiative, carrying China's breadth and depth to the world. Zhang Hao (張皓), Vice-Chair of the Nanping Political Consultative Conference, spoke of compatriots from both sides of the Strait blending together “in a cup of tea”, and of building a shared spiritual home for the Chinese nation.

Lian Ji (連輯), former President of the Chinese National Academy of Arts, recalled that in the winter of 2022, with material from fifteen provinces and forty-four institutions, Chinese tea-making skills and related customs were inscribed on the UNESCO intangible heritage list — a sign, he said, of tea culture becoming the culture of all humanity. Venerable Yangli (養立法師), President of the China Chan Tea Society, offered a poem in celebration:

“Wind and mist all clear on this immortal mountain; layered peaks racing past — a sight to behold. I climb again, still delighted; empty of stray thoughts, a distinct, pure joy.”

Venerable Tiankong (天空法師), Vice-President and Secretary-General of the Canadian International Chan Tea Federation, was among those who spoke in congratulation, and gifts were exchanged between the organising committee and guests, including a gift exchange between the committee's Huang Shenghui (黃聖輝) and Venerable Tiankong, and a palm-leaf sutra and an inscription presented by Liao Linlizhen (廖林麗貞).

Performances and the afternoon exchange

The opening was accompanied by performances, including the “Twelve Paths of Tianxin Chan Tea” tea ceremony, singing, dance — among them a Dunhuang flying-apsaras dance — martial arts, and a children's song on kinship across the Strait. That afternoon, a cultural exchange entitled “Tea and the World · Come and Drink Tea — A Cup of Tea for a Healthy Life” was held, with keynote addresses by Venerable Yangli, Professor Liu Zongmin (劉宗民) of Fu Jen Catholic University and Academician Xiong Chengyu (熊澄宇), and discussion among leaders, experts and scholars on harmony of heart, harmony of family and harmony of society, and on shared roots across the Strait, world peace and tea and the world.

Chan and Tea Culture International promotes Chan — the Chinese Buddhist Chan school — and Chinese tea culture. Through tea, the association offers a way into stillness, compassion and harmony: a quiet bowl of tea, shared with others, can help the mind settle, open the heart, and bring people together in peace.

2024年11月17日，以“一山一水一片叶·一带一路一壶茶”为主题的第十二届大红袍禅茶文化交流活动在武夷山举行，主题为“让世界看见中国大红袍”。来自美国、加拿大、法国、日本、德国、澳大利亚、印度等国家的国际友人，以及台湾、澳门地区的同胞相聚武夷，以茶为媒，以和为尚，共襄盛会。

本次活动由第十六届海峡两岸茶业博览会组委会主办，福建省民族与宗教事务厅、南平市民族与宗教事务局、武夷山市民族与宗教事务局指导，福建省禅茶文化交流协会、武夷山市天心诸子文化交流中心、武夷山市禅茶文化研究会承办。武夷山是乌龙茶和红茶的发源地，“万里茶道”起点，首个“中国茶文化艺术之乡”，国家首批非物质文化遗产武夷岩茶（大红袍）制作技艺的所在地。

开幕致辞

福建省政协原副主席、海峡两岸茶叶交流协会会长林钟乐首先致欢迎辞。他说，武夷山茶已不仅是饮品，更是中国文化的象征和“一带一路”倡议中的“绿色名片”，通过茶香将中国的博大精深带向世界，成为连接中外、沟通文化的桥梁。南平市政协副主席张皓致辞表示，本次活动彰显海峡两岸在一杯茶中交融着“你中有我，我中有你”的同胞情谊，在闽台交往交流交融中构筑中华民族共有精神家园。

中国艺术研究院原院长连辑在致辞中说，2022年的冬天，我国15个省参与、44个单位提供相关素材，让茶生产技艺和相关习俗入列世界非物质文化遗产，这是中国文化走向世界的重要标志，让茶文化由中国的文化变成了世界的文化，变成了全人类的文化。全国人大代表、中国茶禅学会理事长养立法师作诗一首：

“风烟俱净此仙山，叠嶂飞驰是可观。吾复登临仍胜意，空无杂念别清欢。”

加拿大国际禅茶文化联合会副会长兼秘书长天空法师等致辞祝贺。随后互赠礼物，包括组委会代表黄圣辉与天空法师互赠礼物，台湾财团法人廖正豪·廖林丽贞向阳基金会董事长廖林丽贞向组委会赠送贝叶经和题词等。

表演与下午交流

开幕式上穿插了精彩的节目表演，包括《天心禅茶十二道》禅茶表演、歌唱、舞蹈《敦煌飞天》、武术表演和《大美武夷》等。17日下午，举行了2024武夷山“茶和天下·喫茶去——健康人生一杯茶”文化交流活动，养立法师、辅仁大学刘宗民教授、熊澄宇院士作主旨发言，各界领导、专家、学者畅谈人心和悦、家庭和睦、社会和谐，并共话两岸合源、世界和平、茶和天下。

国际禅茶文化联合会致力于弘扬禅——中国佛教禅宗——与中国茶文化。通过一杯茶，协会希望为大众带来宁静、慈悲与和谐：一碗静静分享的茶，可以帮助心安定下来，打开心胸，让人们在和平中共聚。
