TIANXIN TEMPLE, WUYISHAN — 10 SEPTEMBER 2026武夷山天心寺 — 2026年9月10日

Learning the Chan Tea Arrangement at Tianxin Temple, Wuyishan, 10 September 2026學習禪茶擺置：參訪武夷山天心永樂禪寺，2026年9月10日

On 10 September 2026, members of the International Chan and Tea Culture Association visited Tianxin Temple in Wuyishan. There, lay practitioners Yinxiang and Tianxi led everyone in learning the Chan tea arrangement method: how Chan tea is laid out when it is served and when guests are received.

Learning the arrangement

The group studied the arrangements used in the temple tea rooms, looking closely at how each tea setting is laid out: where each vessel is placed, and how the whole setting is ordered. All of the photographs of the settings were sent to everyone afterwards, so that the arrangements could be studied again and learned from in the future.

Remembering by heart

Everyone was asked to commit all the arrangement methods to memory. The purpose is practical: whenever Chan tea must be arranged, or guests received, all will be able to help the master and the monastery, setting out the tea properly and receiving guests well.

2026年9月10日，國際禪茶文化聯合會成員參訪武夷山天心永樂禪寺。印象居士、天溪居士帶領大家學習禪茶擺置佈置法：奉茶與接待賓客時，禪茶如何佈置。

學習擺置法

大家仔細學習了寺內茶室的各種擺置，觀察每件茶具如何安放，整個茶席如何佈置。所有的照片其後都傳給了大家，以便日後再次學習，作為未來學習的參考。

銘記在心

大家應將所有的佈置法銘記在心。其用意十分實際：日後凡需佈置禪茶、接待賓客之時，人人都能幫忙師父、道場，將茶席佈置得宜，接待賓客妥當。
