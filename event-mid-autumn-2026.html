WUTAI SHAN BUDDHIST GARDEN — 25 September 2026加國五台——2026年9月25日

Mid-Autumn Festival Evening, 25 September 2026中秋晚會，2026年9月25日

With gratitude at the heart of the evening, Chan and Tea Culture International assisted the Mid-Autumn Festival at Wutai Shan Buddhist Garden on 25 September with a Chan tea performance and a blind tea-tasting competition — dou cha (鬥茶) — built around Da Hong Pao (大紅袍) rock tea.

The Mid-Autumn Festival is a long-held tradition in China and in countries across East Asia where Chinese culture has taken root. The evening was held in a spirit of gratitude, with tea tasting at its centre and the much-anticipated tea competition as its highlight.

Vice-President Venerable Daojing (道憬法師) welcomed the gathering, including Most Venerable Dayi (達義大和尚), Bhante Mudita, Jinba Lama (金巴喇嗇), Venerable Tiankong (天空法師), Venerable Mingzang (明藏法師) — the nephew-disciple of Most Venerable Dayi. Together they represented the Mahayana, Theravada, and Tibetan Buddhist traditions.

The programme

The programme, drawn up by Venerable Daojing, opened with a ten-minute Chan Tea performance by Annie, followed by a fifteen-minute tea competition in which Vice-President Annie and Franco brewed two teas for blind tasting. Venerable Daojing then offered congratulatory remarks and wishing everybody a happy Mid-Autumn’s Festival and led everyone in offering good wishes to the Most Venerable Dayi Shi and all the Venerables as well as all practitioners for a year of fruitful practice and growth in bodhi, and the Most Venerable Dayi Shi spoke. The closing remarks together took about five minutes.

Chan and Tea Culture International promotes Chan — the Chinese Buddhist Chan school — and Chinese tea culture. Through tea, the association offers a way into stillness, compassion and harmony: a quiet bowl of tea, shared with others, can help the mind settle, open the heart, and bring people together in peace.

懷著感恩的心，國際禪茶文化聯合會於9月25日在加國五台山佛教花園協助舉辦中秋晚會，以禪茶表演和盲品鬥茶比賽為中心，品評的茶是大紅袍巖茶。

中秋節是中國及東亞各地華人文化扎根的國家的悠久傳統。當晚在感恩的氛圍中舉行，以品茶為中心，眾人期待的鬥茶比賽是晚會的亮點。

副會長道憬法師歡迎與會大眾，包括達義大和尚、Bhante Mudita、金巴喇嗇、天空法師、明藏法師——達義大和尚的師姪。他們分別代表漢傳、南傳與藏傳佛教傳統。

晚會流程

晚會流程由道憬法師擬定：先由Annie表演禪茶十分鐘，接著由副會長Annie與Franco進行十五分鐘鬥茶比賽，盲品兩款茶。道憬法師隨後致賀詞，祝大家中秋節快樂，並帶領大家向達義大和尚、諸位法師及所有修行者致上良好祝願，祝願大家今年道業豐碩、菩提增長；達義大和尚亦發言。結語部分合共約五分鐘。

國際禪茶文化聯合會致力於弘揚禪——中國佛教禪宗——與中國茶文化。透過一杯茶，協會希望為大眾帶來寧靜、慈悲與和諧：一碗靜靜分享的茶，可以幫助心安定下來，打開心胸，讓人們在和平中共聚。
